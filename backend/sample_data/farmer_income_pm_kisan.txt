FARMER INCOME SUPPORT SCHEME
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) - Ministry of Agriculture

OVERVIEW:
PM-KISAN is a central sector scheme that provides income support to all landholding farmer families in the country. It aims to supplement their financial needs for procuring agricultural inputs and domestic needs.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a farmer family with cultivable landholding in their names.
- Must be an Indian citizen.
- Must have a valid Aadhaar number.
- Bank account must be linked with Aadhaar.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Small, Marginal, and Large farmers (no landholding limit, earlier limited to 2 hectares).
- Families comprising husband, wife, and minor children.

EXCLUSIONS (Cannot Apply):
- Institutional landholders.
- Present/former holders of constitutional posts, Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors.
- Serving/retired government employees (except Group D/Multi Tasking Staff).
- Pensioners with monthly pension of INR 10,000 or more.
- Income Tax payers in the last assessment year.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, CAs.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card.
2. Land Ownership Documents (Khasra/Khatauni number).
3. Bank Account Passbook (Aadhaar-seeded).
4. Mobile Number.
5. Citizenship Proof.

BENEFITS:
- Financial benefit of INR 6,000 per year per family.
- Payable in three equal installments of INR 2,000 each.
- Installments released every four months (April-July, August-Nov, Dec-March).
- Amount transferred directly to bank accounts (DBT).

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Self-registration online at pmkisan.gov.in under "New Farmer Registration".
2. Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) for assisted registration.
3. Contact local Revenue Officials (Patwari) or Nodal Officer.
4. Use PM-KISAN Mobile App.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Visit portal and verify Aadhaar number.
2. Fill in personal and land details (State, District, Village, Khasra No, Area).
3. Upload supporting land documents and Aadhaar.
4. Save the application form.
5. State/UT government verifies the eligibility and land records.
6. Complete e-KYC (OTP or Biometric) to receive payments.
