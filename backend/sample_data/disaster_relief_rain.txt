DISASTER RELIEF FOR CROP LOSS DUE TO RAIN/FLOOD
State Disaster Relief Fund (SDRF) - Agricultural Assistance

OVERVIEW:
This disaster relief scheme provides immediate financial assistance to farmers who have suffered crop loss due to natural calamities like heavy rainfall, floods, hailstorm, cyclone, and other weather-related disasters. Relief is provided through State Disaster Relief Fund as per SDRF norms.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a farmer (owner cultivator or registered tenant)
- Crop damage must be due to notified natural disaster
- Damage must be 33% or more of the standing crop
- Land must be in officially declared disaster-affected area
- Report damage within 15 days of disaster occurrence
- Must have cultivated the affected land in current season

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Small and marginal farmers (priority)
- Tenant farmers with valid agreement
- Share croppers registered with revenue department
- Agricultural laborers (for separate assistance)
- Farmers whose crops were damaged in last 30 days due to rain, flood, or hailstorm

TYPES OF ASSISTANCE:
For Crop Loss:
- Rainfed crops: INR 6,800 per hectare (limited to 2 hectares)
- Irrigated crops: INR 13,500 per hectare (limited to 2 hectares)
- Perennial crops: INR 18,000 per hectare (limited to 2 hectares)

For Input Subsidy:
- Seeds, fertilizers, pesticides assistance
- Based on actual loss assessment

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Farmer ID Card or Kisan Registration
2. Land ownership records (Patta/Khata/7/12 extract)
3. Aadhaar Card
4. Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
5. Photographs of damaged crop (with date stamp if possible)
6. Damage certificate from Gram Panchayat/Patwari
7. Assessment report from Agriculture Officer
8. Self-declaration of crop sown and area affected

HOW TO GET DAMAGE CERTIFICATE:
1. Report crop damage to Gram Panchayat within 72 hours
2. Patwari/Lekhpal will visit for preliminary assessment
3. Agriculture Officer conducts detailed survey
4. Damage certificate issued based on assessment
5. Certificate specifies percentage of crop loss

RELIEF PROCESS:
1. Disaster declared by District Collector/State Government
2. Joint survey by Revenue and Agriculture departments
3. Beneficiary list prepared at village level
4. Verification by Block Development Officer
5. Approval by District Level Committee
6. Relief amount transferred to bank account

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Report damage immediately to Gram Panchayat
2. Contact Patwari/Village Level Revenue Officer
3. Visit Block Development Office for status
4. Apply through State Disaster Relief portal (if available)
5. Contact District Agriculture Officer for assistance

TIMELINE:
- Report within 72 hours of disaster
- Assessment within 7-10 days
- Relief disbursement within 30-45 days of approval
- Special camps organized in severely affected areas

ADDITIONAL SUPPORT:
- Ex-gratia for loss of life: INR 4,00,000
- Cattle loss compensation: INR 30,000-40,000 per animal
- House damage assistance: As per SDRF norms
- Input subsidy for next season sowing

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Keep photographs as evidence
- Do not clear damaged crop before assessment
- Ensure land records are updated
- Bank account must be active and Aadhaar-linked
