OLD AGE PENSION SCHEME
National Social Assistance Programme - Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

OVERVIEW:
The Old Age Pension Scheme provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens who are living below poverty line. This scheme ensures dignity and basic sustenance for senior citizens who have no regular source of income.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Age: 60 years and above
- Must belong to Below Poverty Line (BPL) family
- Resident of the state for at least 5 years
- Annual family income should not exceed INR 1,00,000
- Should not be receiving any other government pension
- Not employed in government/semi-government organization

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Senior citizens (60+) from BPL families
- Destitute elderly without family support
- Elderly widows/widowers meeting income criteria
- Disabled elderly persons (separate enhanced rate available)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Age proof (Birth certificate/School leaving certificate/Aadhaar)
2. Aadhaar Card
3. BPL Card or Income Certificate (from Tehsildar)
4. Residence proof (Voter ID/Ration Card/Electricity Bill)
5. Bank account passbook (Aadhaar-linked)
6. Recent passport size photograph (2 copies)
7. Self-declaration of no other pension

BENEFITS:
- Central contribution: INR 200-500 per month (age-based)
- State contribution: Additional INR 200-1000 per month (varies by state)
- Higher pension for those aged 80+ (enhanced rate)
- Direct Benefit Transfer to bank account
- No deduction for expenses

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office
2. Apply online through state social welfare portal
3. Submit application at nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
4. Contact local Anganwadi worker for assistance

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Collect application form from Gram Panchayat
2. Fill form with required details
3. Attach all supporting documents
4. Submit to BDO/Sub-Divisional Officer
5. Verification by local authorities
6. Approval and pension disbursement starts

RENEWAL:
- Annual life certificate (Jeevan Pramaan) required
- Can be submitted online or at bank branch
- Pension may be stopped if not renewed
