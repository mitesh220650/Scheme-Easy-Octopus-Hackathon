HOUSING SUBSIDY SCHEME
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) - Housing for All

OVERVIEW:
The Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana provides affordable housing assistance to economically weaker sections, low income groups, and middle income groups. The scheme offers interest subsidy on home loans and direct assistance for house construction.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Economically Weaker Section (EWS): Annual household income up to INR 3,00,000
- Low Income Group (LIG): Annual income INR 3,00,001 to 6,00,000
- Middle Income Group I (MIG-I): Annual income INR 6,00,001 to 12,00,000
- Middle Income Group II (MIG-II): Annual income INR 12,00,001 to 18,00,000
- Beneficiary family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India
- First-time home buyer or family without own house
- Women ownership/co-ownership mandatory for EWS/LIG

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Families without a pucca house
- Families living in kutcha/dilapidated houses
- Slum dwellers for in-situ redevelopment
- First-time home buyers
- Priority: Widows, SC/ST, minorities, disabled persons

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card (all family members)
2. Income certificate from competent authority
3. Proof of no pucca house ownership (self-declaration/affidavit)
4. Land ownership documents (for individual house construction)
5. Bank account details (Aadhaar-linked)
6. Passport size photographs
7. Caste certificate (if applicable for priority)
8. PAN Card (for MIG categories)
9. Property documents (for purchase/construction)

BENEFITS:
EWS Category:
- Direct assistance: INR 1,00,000 to 1,50,000 for house construction
- Interest subsidy: 6.5% on loan up to INR 6 lakhs

LIG Category:
- Interest subsidy: 6.5% on loan up to INR 6 lakhs
- Net present value benefit: Up to INR 2.67 lakhs

MIG-I Category:
- Interest subsidy: 4% on loan up to INR 9 lakhs
- Net present value benefit: Up to INR 2.35 lakhs

MIG-II Category:
- Interest subsidy: 3% on loan up to INR 12 lakhs
- Net present value benefit: Up to INR 2.30 lakhs

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC)
2. Apply online at pmaymis.gov.in
3. Apply through participating banks/housing finance companies
4. Visit Urban Local Body (ULB) or Municipal Office
5. Contact State Level Nodal Agency (SLNA)

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Check eligibility on PMAY website
2. Fill online application with Aadhaar details
3. Upload required documents
4. Application verified by ULB
5. Approval and subsidy released through bank

FOR RURAL AREAS (PMAY-Gramin):
- Apply through Gram Panchayat
- Selection based on SECC data
- Priority to houseless and kutcha house dwellers
- Direct assistance: INR 1,20,000 (plains) / INR 1,30,000 (hilly areas)
