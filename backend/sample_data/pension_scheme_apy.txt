ATAL PENSION YOJANA
Social Security Scheme for Unorganized Sector - PFRDA

OVERVIEW:
Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme primarily targeted at the unorganized sector. It addresses longevity risks and encourages voluntary saving for retirement.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a citizen of India.
- Age must be between 18 and 40 years.
- Must have a valid savings bank account or post office savings account.
- Should not be an income tax payer (applicable from Oct 1, 2022).

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Workers in the unorganized sector.
- Self-employed individuals.
- Any Indian citizen meeting the age criteria (18-40).

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card (for KYC).
2. Active Savings Bank Account or Post Office Account number.
3. Mobile Number (registered with the bank account).
4. Nominee details (Spouse/Family member).

BENEFITS:
- Guaranteed minimum monthly pension of INR 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, or 5000 at the age of 60 years.
- Pension amount depends on the contribution made during the working years.
- Spouse receives the same guaranteed pension after the subscriber's death.
- Return of full pension corpus to the nominee after the death of both subscriber and spouse.
- Tax benefits under Section 80CCD(1).

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit the bank branch or post office where you have a savings account.
2. Apply online via Net Banking (if offered by your bank).
3. Apply via eNPS portal (enps.nsdl.com).

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Fill the APY registration form with account and nominee details.
2. Authorize the bank for auto-debit of monthly/quarterly/half-yearly contributions.
3. Contribution amount is decided based on entry age and desired pension amount.
4. Ensure sufficient balance in the account for auto-debit to avoid penalties.
5. Receive PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number) and periodic statements.

TIMELINE:
- Contribution Period: From age of joining (18-40) until age 60 (min 20 years).
- Pension Start: After attaining 60 years of age.

CONTACT:
