RURAL EMPLOYMENT GUARANTEE SCHEME
Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

OVERVIEW:
MGNREGA is an employment scheme that guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work. It aims to enhance livelihood security in rural areas.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a citizen of India.
- Must be residing in a rural area.
- Must be at least 18 years of age.
- Must be willing to do unskilled manual work.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Adult members of any rural household.
- Marginalized sections including SC/ST households.
- Women (priority is given such that at least one-third beneficiaries are women).
- People with disabilities (special provisions for suitable work).

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Application for Registration (oral or written).
2. Aadhaar Card.
3. Ration Card (proof of local residence).
4. Bank or Post Office Account details (for wage transfer).
5. Passport size photograph of applicant(s).
6. Voter ID / PAN Card (for identity verification).
7. Job Card (once issued) to apply for work.

BENEFITS:
- Guaranteed 100 days of wage employment per year per household.
- Wages paid directly to bank/post office account within 15 days.
- Work provided within 5 km radius of the village (or 10% extra wage if further).
- Unemployment allowance if work is not provided within 15 days of application.
- Equal wages for men and women.
- Workplace facilities like drinking water, shade, and first-aid.

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit the local Gram Panchayat office.
2. Submit application for registration (names, ages, Aadhaar of adult members).
3. Apply orally or in writing to the Panchayat Secretary or Gram Rozgar Sahayak.
4. Can also apply through the Janmanrega Mobile App.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Submit registration application to Gram Panchayat.
2. Panchayat verifies household and residency details within 14 days.
3. Job Card (JC) is issued to the household (valid for 5 years).
4. Submit "Application for Work" whenever employment is needed (min 14 days work).
5. Panchayat allocates work within 15 days.

- Visit: Gram Panchayat Office or Block Programme Officer.
