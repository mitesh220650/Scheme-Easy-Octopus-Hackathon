CROP INSURANCE RELIEF SCHEME
Scheme for Small and Marginal Farmers

OVERVIEW:
The Crop Insurance Relief Scheme provides financial protection to farmers against crop loss due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases. This scheme aims to stabilize farm income and encourage farmers to adopt innovative agricultural practices.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must be a registered farmer with valid Farmer ID
- Landholding must be less than 5 acres (small farmer) or less than 2.5 acres (marginal farmer)
- Crops must be registered under the scheme before sowing season
- Land must be in notified area for the crop season
- Age: 18 years and above
- Must have a valid bank account linked to Aadhaar

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Owner cultivators
- Tenant farmers with valid tenancy agreement
- Share croppers with landowner consent
- Farmers cultivating on leased land with proper documentation

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Farmer ID Card (Kisan Credit Card or equivalent)
2. Land ownership records (Patta/Khata/7/12 extract)
3. Aadhaar Card (linked to bank account)
4. Bank passbook (first page copy)
5. Sowing certificate from Patwari/Lekhpal
6. Crop damage photographs (if claiming for loss)
7. Damage assessment certificate from Agriculture Officer

BENEFITS:
- Premium subsidy: Government pays 50% of premium for food crops
- Sum insured: Based on threshold yield and minimum support price
- Quick claim settlement within 45 days of harvest
- Coverage for prevented sowing, mid-season adversity, post-harvest losses

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit your nearest Gram Panchayat or Common Service Centre (CSC)
2. Apply online at agri-insurance.gov.in
3. Contact your bank branch if you have a Kisan Credit Card
4. Submit application before sowing season deadline

CLAIM PROCESS:
- Report crop loss within 72 hours to insurance company or agriculture department
- Get damage assessment done by authorized officer
- Submit claim form with required photographs and certificates
- Receive payment directly in bank account
