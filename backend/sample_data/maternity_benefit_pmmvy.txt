MATERNITY BENEFIT SCHEME
Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) - Ministry of Women and Child Development

OVERVIEW:
Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a maternity benefit scheme that provides cash incentives to pregnant women and lactating mothers. The scheme compensates for wage loss, allowing women to take adequate rest before and after delivery, and improves health-seeking behavior.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers (PW&LM) for the first living child.
- For the second child, the benefit is provided only if it is a girl child.
- Must be at least 19 years old.
- Must belong to socially and economically disadvantaged sections (SC/ST, BPL, E-Shram card holders, MGNREGA job card holders, Kisan Samman Nidhi beneficiaries).
- Not applicable to those in regular employment with Central/State Govt or PSUs.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Pregnant women for their first live birth.
- Mothers of a second child (if it is a girl).
- Women with net family income less than INR 8 Lakh per annum.
- Women holding MGNREGA Job Card or E-Shram Card.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card of the beneficiary.
2. Aadhaar-seeded Bank or Post Office Account details.
3. MCP (Mother and Child Protection) Card with LMP and ANC dates.
4. Child Birth Certificate (for second instalment).
5. Eligibility Proof (e.g., BPL Card, MGNREGA Job Card, SC/ST Certificate).
6. Mobile Number.

BENEFITS:
First Child (Total INR 5,000 in 2 instalments):
- 1st Instalment: INR 3,000 on registration of pregnancy and one antenatal checkup within 6 months from LMP.
- 2nd Instalment: INR 2,000 after child birth registration and first cycle of vaccinations (BCG, OPV, DPT, Hepatitis-B).
- Additional INR 1,000 is often available under Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) for institutional delivery.

Second Child (Girl):
- Single Instalment: INR 6,000 after birth registration and vaccinations.

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Online via PMMVY Portal (pmmvy.wcd.gov.in) or UMANG App.
2. Offline at the nearest Anganwadi Centre (AWC) or approved Health Facility.
3. Submit Form 1-A for registration and first instalment.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Register at Anganwadi Centre or online portal using mobile number.
2. Submit Aadhaar and eligibility documents.
3. For offline: Anganwadi Worker (AWW) assists in filling the form.
4. For second instalment: Submit proof of child birth and immunization.
5. Benefits are transferred directly to the bank account (DBT).

CONTACT:
- Helpline: 14555 (check state-specific numbers).
- Website: pmmvy.wcd.gov.in
- Visit: Nearest Anganwadi Centre or CDPO Office.
