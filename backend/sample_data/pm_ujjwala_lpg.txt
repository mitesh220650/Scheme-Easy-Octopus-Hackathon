FREE LPG CONNECTION SCHEME
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) - Clean Fuel, Better Life

OVERVIEW:
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY 2.0) provides clean cooking fuel (LPG) to women from poor households. The scheme provides a deposit-free LPG connection to eligible households to prevent health hazards from smoke.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Applicant must be an adult woman (18+ years).
- Must belong to a poor household (SC, ST, PMAY, AAY, MBC, Forest Dwellers, or SECC list).
- There should be no other LPG connection in the same household.
- Must verify status via deprivation declaration or ration card.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Women from SC/ST households.
- Beneficiaries of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin).
- Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) beneficiaries.
- Most Backward Classes (MBC).
- Tea and Ex-Tea Garden Tribes.
- Forest Dwellers and people in River Islands.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Know Your Customer (KYC) Form.
2. Aadhaar Card of applicant (Proof of Identity and Address).
3. Aadhaar Cards of all adult family members.
4. Ration Card or State Family Composition Document.
5. Bank Account Passbook and IFSC Code.
6. Passport size photograph.
7. Self-declaration for migrant workers (if ration card address doesn't match).

BENEFITS:
- Deposit-free LPG connection (Security deposit covered by Govt).
- Free first LPG refill.
- Free Gas Stove (Hotplate) provided with connection.
- Financial assistance of up to INR 1600 per connection (for cylinder/regulator security).
- Cash assistance for pipe and installation charges.

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit the nearest LPG Distributor (Indane, Bharat Gas, HP Gas).
2. Apply online at www.pmuy.gov.in portal.
3. Visit a Common Service Centre (CSC).

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Fill the KYC application form.
2. Submit form with Aadhaar, Ration Card, and Bank details to distributor.
3. Oil Marketing Company (OMC) performs de-duplication check (to ensure no existing connection).
4. Upon approval, the connection is released to the beneficiary.
