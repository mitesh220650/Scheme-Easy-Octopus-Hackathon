STUDENT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME
Pre-Matric and Post-Matric Scholarship for SC/ST/OBC/Minority Students

OVERVIEW:
This scholarship scheme provides financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Other Backward Classes (OBC), and Minority communities to support their education from Class 1 to Post-Graduation level.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Must belong to SC/ST/OBC/Minority community
- Family annual income should not exceed INR 2,50,000 (varies by category)
- Must be a regular student enrolled in recognized institution
- Minimum 50% attendance required
- Should not be receiving any other scholarship simultaneously
- Must have passed previous examination

WHO CAN APPLY:
- SC/ST students from Class 1 to Post-Graduation
- OBC students with family income below limit
- Minority students (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi)
- Students with disabilities (additional support available)

PRE-MATRIC (Class 1-10):
- For students in Class 1 to Class 10
- Day scholars and hostellers eligible
- Income limit: INR 2,50,000 per annum

POST-MATRIC (Class 11 onwards):
- For students from Class 11 to Post-Graduation
- Professional courses included
- Higher scholarship for hostellers

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Caste Certificate (issued by Tehsildar/SDM)
2. Income Certificate (current financial year)
3. Aadhaar Card (student and parent/guardian)
4. Previous year marksheet/passing certificate
5. Current year admission receipt/bonafide certificate
6. Bank account passbook (student's own account)
7. Passport size photograph
8. Institution verification certificate
9. Fee receipt of current academic year

BENEFITS:
Pre-Matric:
- Maintenance allowance: INR 150-350 per month
- Ad-hoc grant: INR 750 per annum
- Book grant included

Post-Matric:
- Full tuition fee reimbursement (for government institutions)
- Maintenance allowance: INR 550-1200 per month
- Study tour and thesis allowance for research students

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Apply online at National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in)
2. Fresh application: Before October 31
3. Renewal: Before November 30
4. Institute verification required
5. Final approval by State Welfare Department

APPLICATION STEPS:
1. Register on National Scholarship Portal
2. Fill personal, academic, and bank details
3. Upload required documents
4. Submit for institute verification
5. Institute forwards to District/State office
6. Approval and disbursement to bank account
