AYUSHMAN BHARAT SCHEME
Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) - National Health Authority

OVERVIEW:
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest health assurance scheme which aims at providing a health cover of INR 5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization. The scheme covers over 10.74 crore poor and vulnerable families.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Families identified under Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data.
- Rural: Households with kucha walls/roof, no adult member 16-59 years, landless households deriving income from casual labor, destitute, manual scavengers, SC/ST households.
- Urban: Occupational categories like ragpickers, beggars, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers.
- Senior Citizens: All citizens aged 70 years and above irrespective of income status.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Members of eligible families as per SECC database.
- Senior citizens aged 70+ years.
- Families with no able-bodied adult members.
- Priority given to girl child, women, and senior citizens.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Aadhaar Card (mandatory for identity).
2. Ration Card or Family ID (to establish family relationships).
3. Mobile Number (linked with Aadhaar).
4. Caste Certificate (if applying under SC/ST category).
5. Income Certificate (if applicable for specific state schemes).
6. PM Letter (if received by the family).

BENEFITS:
- Health coverage up to INR 5,00,000 per family per year.
- Cashless and paperless access to services at empanelled hospitals.
- Covers 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses (diagnostics, medicines).
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender.
- All pre-existing diseases are covered from day one.
- Portable benefits across India.

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Check eligibility online at mera.pmjay.gov.in ("Am I Eligible" portal).
2. Visit nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or Empanelled Hospital.
3. Contact Ayushman Mitra at the hospital help desk.
4. Senior citizens can apply via the Ayushman App or beneficiary portal.

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Beneficiary visits hospital or CSC with Aadhaar and Ration Card.
2. Arogya Mitra verifies eligibility in the BIS (Beneficiary Identification System).
3. e-KYC is performed using Aadhaar authentication.
4. Documents are scanned and uploaded.
5. On approval, a Golden Record is generated and the e-Card is issued.
