MICRO LOAN FOR SMALL BUSINESS
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) - Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency

OVERVIEW:
MUDRA Yojana provides loans up to INR 10 lakhs to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises. The scheme supports small business owners, artisans, street vendors, and entrepreneurs to start or expand their businesses without collateral requirement.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA:
- Indian citizen aged 18 years and above
- Should have a business plan or existing small business
- Non-farm income generating activities
- Manufacturing, trading, or service sector enterprises
- No collateral required for loans up to INR 10 lakhs
- No previous default with any bank/financial institution

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Small shopkeepers and traders
- Fruit and vegetable vendors
- Artisans and craftsmen
- Food processing units (papad, pickle, etc.)
- Tailors, beauty parlors, salons
- Auto rickshaw/taxi drivers (for vehicle purchase)
- Small manufacturing units
- Repair shops (mobile, electronics, vehicles)
- Self-employed professionals

LOAN CATEGORIES:
SHISHU:
- Loan amount: Up to INR 50,000
- For starting/early stage businesses
- Lowest interest rates

KISHORE:
- Loan amount: INR 50,001 to INR 5,00,000
- For established businesses seeking expansion
- Working capital and equipment finance

TARUN:
- Loan amount: INR 5,00,001 to INR 10,00,000
- For well-established businesses
- Major expansion and modernization

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:
1. Identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN/Passport)
2. Address proof (Utility bill/Aadhaar/Rent agreement)
3. Passport size photographs (2-3 copies)
4. Business proof (License/Registration/GST if applicable)
5. Bank statements (last 6 months if existing account)
6. Project report/Business plan (for larger loans)
7. Quotations for machinery/equipment (if purchasing)
8. Caste certificate (for priority lending)
9. Category certificate for women/SC/ST (additional benefits)

BENEFITS:
- No collateral/security required
- Low interest rates (as per bank guidelines)
- Flexible repayment tenure (3-5 years)
- MUDRA Card for working capital (like credit card)
- Additional subsidy under Stand-Up India for SC/ST/Women
- Interest subvention under various state schemes

HOW TO APPLY:
1. Visit any Public Sector Bank, Private Bank, or MFI
2. Apply online at mudra.org.in or udyamimitra.in
3. Contact nearest bank branch with business plan
4. Apply through Common Service Centre (CSC)
5. NBFCs and Micro Finance Institutions also provide MUDRA loans

APPLICATION PROCESS:
1. Prepare business plan/project report
2. Visit bank with required documents
3. Fill MUDRA loan application form
4. Bank verifies documents and business viability
5. Loan sanctioned and disbursed (usually within 7-10 days)
6. Repay through EMIs as per agreed schedule

SPECIAL PROVISIONS:
- Women entrepreneurs: Priority lending and lower rates
- SC/ST entrepreneurs: Additional government subsidy available
- Existing borrowers: Can apply for higher category loan after successful repayment
